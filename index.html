01 / Selected research
Research / 01Dexterous manipulation

From a human hand to a robot hand.

My glove project connects wearable motion sensing to a simulated robotic hand. The system uses 11 IMUs across the wrist and fingers to drive a 16-DOF hand in MuJoCo.

IMU sensingMuJoCoArduinoMotion mapping
Discuss this work
System
Arduino Mega 2560 and a CD74HC4067 multiplexer form the sensor acquisition pipeline.
Engineering focus
Sensor synchronization, serial bandwidth and filtering affect latency and motion jitter.
Research interest
How can captured human motion become useful control input for dexterous manipulation?

Research / 02Human–robot interaction

When should a robot respond?

My research interests also include conversational turn-taking: how a robot can time its response during an interaction with a person.

Turn-takingConversational timingHuman–robot interaction
Discuss this work
Context
Undergraduate research at HKUST, exploring conversational timing in human–robot interaction.
Research question
What signals help a system decide when to keep listening and when to take a turn?
Connection to robotics
Response timing is part of how a robot behaves around people, alongside perception and physical action.
