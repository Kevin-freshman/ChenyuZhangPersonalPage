Research 01 Ongoing

Dexterous Teleoperation
for Robotic Hands

Undergraduate robotics research · HKUST

Developing a wearable sensing system that maps natural wrist and finger movement to a simulated 16-DOF robotic hand in MuJoCo.

  • Integrate 11 IMUs distributed across the wrist and fingers.
  • Build the acquisition pipeline with Arduino Mega 2560 and a CD74HC4067 multiplexer.
  • Study sensor synchronization, serial bandwidth, filtering, latency, and motion jitter.
MuJoCo Arduino IMU sensing Motion mapping
